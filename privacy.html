Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Abiso Solutions, the software developer based in the Republic of the Philippines operating and publishing software ("Developer," "we," "us," or "our"), acts as the Personal Information Controller (PIC) and collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Abiso Clicker Pro Android mobile application, Windows Host desktop application, and browser extensions (collectively, the "Service").

Abiso Clicker Pro is built on an offline-first, local-network architecture: your presentation slides, speaker notes, and live laser/spotlight coordinates travel strictly across your local Wi-Fi network between your phone and computer and are never uploaded to our servers. Cloud services (Google Firebase and Google Play Billing) are used strictly to authenticate accounts, prevent trial exploitation while an account ledger exists, and maintain your licensing entitlement ledger.

1. Information We Collect and Store in the Cloud (Firebase)

To manage user accounts, prevent repeated trial exploitation across app reinstalls, and maintain licensing entitlements, the Service processes the following information through Google Firebase:

A. User-Supplied Information & Firebase Authentication Metadata

  • Email Address (Supplied by User): When you sign in to access the Free Trial, purchase or restore Pro features, or manage your license, you supply your email address (such as via Google Sign-In).
  • Firebase-Generated Authentication Metadata: Firebase Authentication automatically generates a unique authentication user identifier (UID) and maintains standard operational authentication metadata (such as account creation and sign-in timestamps, and basic OAuth token attributes provided by your sign-in method) to authenticate your session securely.

B. App-Collected Device Identifier & Firebase Entitlement Ledger

  • Android Device Identifier (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID — Collected by the App): The Android app collects your device's Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID. On Android 8.0 (API level 26) and higher, this 64-bit hexadecimal string is scoped uniquely to the combination of the app-signing key, Android user profile, and device (it is not a physical hardware serial number, and it persists across ordinary app uninstalls and reinstalls on the same device and Android user profile unless the device is factory-reset). We collect and store this identifier in your Firebase entitlement ledger strictly for:
    1. Preventing abuse and repeated exploitation of the 7-day Free Trial across ordinary app uninstallations and reinstallations while an entitlement record exists; and
    2. Managing device-related licensing and entitlement records, including enforcing the one-active-device limit and enabling the "Reclaim License" feature when transferring a Pro License to another phone.
  • Entitlement Ledger Records: Our Firebase database stores your entitlement ledger, which records your user email/UID, bound ANDROID_ID, Pro license status, and the specific active-use dates consumed during your seven (7) active-use days Free Trial.

2. Information Kept Strictly on Your Local Devices

The following presentation data is processed and stored strictly on your local devices and is never transmitted to our cloud servers:

  • Presentation Content & Speaker Notes: Slide previews, presentation titles, and speaker notes are transmitted directly between your Windows PC and your Android phone over your local Wi-Fi or Hotspot network (TCP Port 8181) using AES-GCM-256 encryption.
  • Real-Time Pointer Coordinates: High-frequency motion and touch coordinates for the digital laser, spotlight, and magnifier are streamed strictly over local UDP (Port 8182) to your Windows PC and discarded in real time.
  • Slide Pacing & Delivery Analytics: Slide duration logs, pacing metrics, and backtrack counts are saved locally on your Android device to generate local PDF Delivery Reports.

3. Browser Extension Data Access (Google Slides & Canva)

If you install the optional Abiso Clicker Pro Browser Extension (for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or compatible Chromium browsers), the extension operates as follows:

  • What Information the Extension Reads: The extension reads the presentation title, current and total slide numbers, and speaker notes text from the webpage Document Object Model (DOM) of supported presentation tabs, plus slide viewport coordinates for laser/spotlight alignment.
  • When It Accesses Information: Access occurs strictly when you have an active presentation open on https://docs.google.com/presentation/* or https://www.canva.com/* and the Windows Host application is actively running on your computer. It does not read or monitor any other browser tabs or browsing history.
  • How Information Is Transferred: Extracted speaker notes and slide synchronization states are sent strictly over your computer's local loopback interface (localhost / 127.0.0.1 WebSocket) to the Abiso Windows Host application, which relays them over your encrypted local Wi-Fi network (AES-GCM-256) to your paired Android phone.
  • No Cloud Transmission: Zero data accessed by the browser extension is ever sent to our servers, Firebase, or any third party.
  • Required Browser Permissions: Host permissions restricted to Google Slides and Canva domains to read speaker notes/slide controls, and local script execution to synchronize presentation commands.

4. QR Code Scanner & Android Device Permissions

The Abiso Clicker Pro Android app uses the following system features and permissions solely for local operational functionality:

Feature / Permission Technical Mechanism & Data Handling
QR Code Pairing (Google Code Scanner API)
(No app CAMERA permission requested)		 Used strictly to scan the pairing QR code displayed on the Windows Host application to retrieve the host computer's local IP address and pairing PIN for establishing a local network connection. Scanning is delegated to Google Play services via Google's Code Scanner API, so Abiso Clicker Pro does not request direct CAMERA permission. Image processing occurs entirely on-device; neither our app nor Google stores camera frames or scan results in the cloud, and connection details are never used for marketing or user tracking.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (and related service types) Maintains an active local TCP/UDP socket connection with your Windows PC while you lock your phone screen or switch apps during a live presentation, displaying a persistent status notification while connected.
WAKE_LOCK (and Wi-Fi Lock mechanisms) Used to acquire a partial CPU wake lock (PowerManager.WakeLock) so the processor continues handling presentation commands when the screen is off, and where applicable in the active connection service, a Wi-Fi lock (WifiManager.WifiLock) to keep the Wi-Fi radio awake during a live presentation session.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback when you advance slides, toggle the laser pointer, or reach presentation timer milestones.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Used to transmit local presentation commands over Wi-Fi/Hotspot (Ports 8181/8182) and to communicate over HTTPS with Firebase and Google Play Billing.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not include any third-party advertising SDKs. The Android application relies only on the following Google LLC services:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Provides cloud authentication and hosts the entitlement ledger database (Privacy & Security in Firebase).
  • Google Play services (Google LLC): Provides core Android system integration, Google Sign-In authentication services, and the on-device Google Code Scanner API used for local QR pairing (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): Processes one-time in-app purchases for the Android application distributed through the Google Play Store. We do not collect, inspect, or store your credit card, bank account, or e-wallet details; all payment credentials are handled directly by Google.

6. Data Retention: Uninstalling the App vs. Deleting Your Account

It is important to understand the difference between uninstalling the Abiso Clicker Pro app from your phone and deleting your account:

Situation What Happens to Your Cloud Data Effect on Restoration & Trial History
1. You Uninstall the App (Without deleting your account) Firebase Entitlement Ledger Remains: Your local app files are removed from your phone, but your cloud entitlement ledger (email/UID, bound ANDROID_ID, trial usage count, and Pro entitlement status) remains stored in Firebase. Same Phone Reinstall: If you reinstall the app on the same phone, your matching ANDROID_ID and account allow seamless restoration of your Pro License or remaining trial state (and prevent resetting an already-consumed trial).

Switching to Another Phone: Because a Pro License can only be active on one device at a time, signing into a different phone will detect your existing ledger binding and allow you to transfer your Pro License to the new phone by tapping "Reclaim License" in the app.
2. You Delete Your Account (In-App or via External Request) Firebase Records Are Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication record and your entire Firebase entitlement ledger (including your stored email, ANDROID_ID, and trial records) are erased from our database. No separate developer-held backup is retained (while standard cloud infrastructure operational logs are governed by Google Firebase's internal lifecycle). Once deletion is completed, our database no longer retains your previous entitlement ledger or historical ANDROID_ID trial record.
  • Method A — Automatic In-App Deletion: Open Abiso Clicker Pro → Settings → Delete Account. Confirming this prompt triggers automatic deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and associated entitlement ledger node, subject to Firebase's standard deletion execution.
  • Method B — External Deletion Request (Without the App Installed): If you have already uninstalled the app and want your remaining Firebase entitlement ledger deleted, visit our External Account Deletion Page (https://abisosolutions.github.io/account-deletion.html) to submit an email request. External requests are completed after verifying email ownership, within seven (7) business days.
  • Restoring Google Play Purchases After Account Deletion: Because Google Play purchases are recorded independently in your Google Play account by Google, if you delete your Abiso account and later sign in again using the same Google Play account, the app can verify your purchase token with Google Play Billing to restore Pro access.

7. Your Data Privacy Rights (Philippine DPA & International Laws)

Under Section 16 of Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012), as well as the EEA/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable international privacy laws, you have the following rights as a Data Subject:

  • Right to be Informed: To be informed whether your personal data is being, or has been, processed, including the existence of automated entitlement checks.
  • Right to Object: To object to the processing of your personal data where processing is based on consent or legitimate interests.
  • Right to Access: To demand reasonable access to the personal data we hold about you, how it was processed, the recipients, and the manner of storage.
  • Right to Rectification (Correction): To dispute any inaccuracy or error in your personal data and have us correct it immediately.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To suspend, withdraw, or order the blocking, removal, or destruction of your personal data from our filing systems (exercisable anytime via the in-app Delete Account button or our Account Deletion page).
  • Right to Data Portability: To obtain an electronic copy of your account and entitlement data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Damages & Right to File a Complaint: To be indemnified for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, or unlawfully obtained/used personal data, and the right to lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines (https://privacy.gov.ph) or with your local data protection supervisory authority.

How to Exercise Your Rights: Send a request from the email address associated with your account to abisosolutions@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request". We will verify your identity and respond within fifteen (15) to thirty (30) calendar days in accordance with applicable statutory timelines.

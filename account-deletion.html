Account & Data Deletion

Application: Abiso Clicker Pro • Developer: Abiso Solutions

You can permanently delete your Abiso Clicker Pro user account and associated cloud records at any time—either directly from inside the Android application or externally through this webpage if you have already uninstalled the app.

1. Important Distinction: Uninstalling the App vs. Deleting Your Account

Action What Happens to Your Firebase Records
Uninstalling the App (Without deleting your account) Your local app files are removed from your phone, but your Firebase entitlement ledger remains stored in the cloud (containing your account email/UID, bound Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID, trial status, and Pro entitlement record). If you reinstall on the same phone, your state is restored automatically; if you switch to a different phone, you can transfer your Pro License to the new device using the in-app "Reclaim License" button.
Deleting Your Account (In-App or via External Request) Your Firebase Authentication record and your entire Firebase entitlement ledger (including your email, ANDROID_ID, and trial records) are erased from our database. No separate developer-held backup is retained (while standard cloud infrastructure operational logs are governed by Google Firebase's internal lifecycle).

2. Two Ways to Delete Your Account

Method A: Automatic In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the Abiso Clicker Pro Android app installed on your phone:

  1. Open Abiso Clicker Pro and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear icon) menu.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. This triggers automatic deletion of your Firebase Authentication record and associated Firebase entitlement ledger, subject to Firebase's standard deletion execution. No separate developer-held backup is retained.

Method B: External Deletion Request (If You Already Uninstalled the App)

If you have already uninstalled the app and want your remaining Firebase entitlement ledger deleted without reinstalling the app:

  1. Send an email from the exact email address associated with your Abiso Clicker Pro account to abisosolutions@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Abiso Clicker Pro.
  3. We will verify ownership of the email address, complete the deletion of your Firebase Authentication and entitlement ledger records within seven (7) business days, and send you a confirmation email once completed. No separate developer-held backup is retained.

3. Restoring a Google Play Purchase After Account Deletion

If you purchased a Lifetime Pro License through the Google Play Store, your purchase token remains recorded by Google in your Google Play account. If you delete your Abiso account and later decide to reinstall Abiso Clicker Pro and sign in again with the same Google Play profile, the app can verify your purchase with Google Play Billing to restore your Pro License.
